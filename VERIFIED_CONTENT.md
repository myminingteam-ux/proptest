- Crypto-focused simulated trading evaluation platform.
- Evaluation and funded-stage accounts use virtual funds.
- No customer deposits are accepted for these trading accounts.
- No customer capital is managed through these accounts.
- Public positioning: approximately / 350+ USDT perpetual markets.
- Maximum public account-size positioning: up to $200K.
- Approved performance-based payouts use USDT under the current model.
- Evaluation accounts currently list no expiry.
